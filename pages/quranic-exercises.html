Practice Exercises

Test and reinforce your understanding of Tajweed rules through interactive exercises. Practice applying the principles you've learned to real Quranic text.

How to Use These Exercises

These exercises are designed to help you apply Tajweed rules to actual Quranic text. Each exercise tests a specific aspect of your learning. Work through them systematically to build comprehensive Tajweed knowledge.

📍 Makhraj Identification

Identify the articulation point of given Arabic letters and explain their characteristics.

⏹️ Waqf Application

Practice identifying proper stopping points in Quranic verses based on waqf rules.

🎵 Pronunciation Quiz

Listen to letter pronunciations and identify which letter is being recited.

📝 Rules Application

Apply specific Tajweed rules to phrases and explain your reasoning.

🔤 Text Analysis

Analyze Quranic passages for Tajweed elements like emphatic letters and nasal sounds.

🎯 Comprehensive Test

A full Tajweed assessment covering all learned principles and applications.
